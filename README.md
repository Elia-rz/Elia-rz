## 👋 Hi, I'm Elia

I study **how people decide to trust AI** — and then I design and build systems that earn it.

I'm a **PhD candidate in Engineering Management at Stevens Institute of Technology**, working at the intersection of **human factors, UX research, and explainable AI**. My work asks a simple question with high stakes: *when an AI gives a recommendation, what does a person need to see to use it well?*

I've explored that question with **experts** (radiologists, oncologists) and **everyday users** (people seeking wellness guidance) — moving from interviews and experiments to working, full-stack prototypes.

```text
Research question  →  Study design  →  Interface prototype  →  User evaluation  →  Design guidance
```
